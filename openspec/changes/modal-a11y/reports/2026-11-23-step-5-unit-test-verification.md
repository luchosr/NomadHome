## Accepted items

- The global `html { scrollbar-gutter: stable }` (`apps/web/src/index.css`) reserves a gutter on every route that shows a classic scrollbar, not only while a dialog is open. Accepted: it is what keeps the page from jumping when the scroll lock removes the scrollbar.
- jsdom cannot model the top layer, inert or `CloseWatcher`; the `showModal` and `close` stubs in `src/test/setup.ts` only toggle `open` and fire `close`. The real behaviour is owned by the e2e suite.
